# 📊 HR Analytics Power BI Dashboard

A comprehensive, multi-page Power BI analytics solution for workforce intelligence — covering salary, attrition, performance, and department-wise metrics across 1,470+ employee records.

## 🗂️ Dashboard Pages

| Page | Focus | Key Metric |
|------|-------|------------|
| 💰 Salary & Compensation | Income by dept, job role, age group, salary slab | Avg monthly income: **6.5K** |
| 📉 Employee Attrition | Attrition by age, travel, job level, satisfaction | Attrition rate: **~16%** |
| 🚀 Performance & Growth | Ratings, training time, tenure vs role years | Avg performance rating: **3.15** |
| 🏢 Department Wise | Income, overtime, work-life balance, headcount | Total monthly income: **10M** |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Sales Executives** generate the highest total monthly income at **2.29M**
- **R&D** is the largest department with **967 employees** vs 450 in Sales and 63 in HR  
- Employees who **travel frequently** show significantly higher attrition rates  
- Performance ratings are remarkably consistent (~3.15) across all salary slabs — no pay-performance gap  
- The **26–45 age group** accounts for the majority of the workforce and income

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Tool:** Power BI Desktop  
- **Language:** DAX, Power Query (M)  
- **Dataset:** HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance  
- **Visuals used:** Bar, Donut, Pie, Waterfall, Scatter, Area, Stacked Bar, KPI Cards
